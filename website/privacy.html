Privacy and pre-order terms

This page is in English so that its meaning is exact.

Draft for review. Have a lawyer check this page before launch.

Who we are

FloodMesh is made by DVerse Labs, India. DVerse Labs decides how the details you give us are used. Contact: +91 93602 47270, support@dverselabs.com.

What we collect

When you send a pre-order: your name, mobile number, email (if you give it), organisation (if you give one), address, city, PIN code, state, any notes, the products and quantities you chose, the page language, and the time. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The site remembers your language and cart in your own browser only.

Why we use it

  • To record your pre-order and call you to confirm it.
  • To tell you when units are ready to ship, or if anything about your pre-order changes.
  • If you ask for responder units, to check that the organisation is a real rescue team, NGO or authority.

We use your details only with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the order form. We do not sell or rent them, and we do not use them for unrelated marketing.

Where it is kept

Pre-orders are stored in a Google Sheet in DVerse Labs' Google account, and we receive an email for each one. Google processes this data for us. Only people at DVerse Labs who handle orders can see it.

How long we keep it

Until your units are delivered or your pre-order is cancelled, and then for as long as the law requires us to keep sales records. If you cancel before anything ships, we delete your details within 30 days unless you ask us to keep you informed.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent cancels the pre-order. To do any of this, or to raise a complaint, contact us at +91 93602 47270 or support@dverselabs.com.

Pre-order terms

  • A pre-order is a request, not a purchase. You pay nothing when you send it.
  • We call you to confirm the products, quantity, price and delivery before anything ships. Nothing is sold until you confirm on that call.
  • FloodMesh units can only be sold once the radio equipment approval and battery registrations required in India are complete. We ship only after that.
  • FloodMesh is still in development. Features, contents and prices may change before shipping; we will tell you before you confirm.
  • You can cancel a pre-order at any time before it ships, at no cost.
  • We set up responder units only for organisations we have checked.
