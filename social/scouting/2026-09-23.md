## Why nothing qualified

Every candidate the searches surfaced fell into one of two buckets:

1. **Too old.** Nearly everything indexed by WebSearch for these queries is
   historical: Chennai 2015, Kerala 2018, Michaung/Dec 2023, Aug 2025 J&K
   fibre-cut outage, an Aug 2025 Hyderabad rain thread, a Scott Hanselman
   Meshtastic-range tweet from Dec 2025, an "off-grid communicator" build
   tweet from Apr 2026, and an "An Introduction to Meshtastic" HN thread
   (and its comment tree) from 13 May 2026. All are well outside the 14-day
   window (9–23 September 2026), so all were dropped regardless of how well
   they otherwise matched.
2. **Routine alerts, not people.** The genuinely current India flood
   activity this window — Hyderabad's 23 Sept flash-flood watch, the 14
   Sept Ahmedabad/Saurashtra rain, Chennai's 18 Sept watch — is carried on
   X almost entirely by official/weather-tracker accounts (GHMC, IMD
   Hyderabad, @Hyderabadrains, HYDRAA) posting routine rainfall bulletins,
   not people describing being cut off or asking a question we can answer.
   Relevance 0 under the "sales post by another company" / off-topic
   equivalent for routine alerts — skipped.

No item reached relevance ≥1 with a date inside the last 14 days, so the
cap-and-score step in `scouting.md` never had a survivor to work with.

## Source reachability — this matters for future runs

The playbook's four direct-fetch methods were all **unreachable**, not just
today's searches coming up dry:

- `cdn.syndication.twimg.com` (X post text/counts) — blocked by the
  environment's network egress proxy (`connect_rejected`, policy denial),
  tried twice.
- `www.instagram.com/p/.../embed/captioned/` — same, blocked.
- `www.reddit.com/search.json` — same, blocked.
- `hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search_by_date` — same, blocked.
- As a fallback, `WebFetch` was tried against `x.com`, `news.ycombinator.com`,
  and several news domains (Deccan Herald, Kashmir Times) used to verify
  candidate dates — all returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`.

`WebSearch` itself still works, but it draws on a stale/indexed snapshot
that skews heavily toward older, already-established content rather than
this week's posts, which compounded the freshness problem above.

Net effect: this run could only ever find candidates it can't verify the
date, text, or engagement numbers for, which is why nothing survived
scoring. This looks like an environment/network-policy issue rather than a
quiet news week, and is worth checking before the next scheduled run.
