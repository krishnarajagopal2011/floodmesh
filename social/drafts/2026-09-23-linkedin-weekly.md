## Post

Our own circuit board is designed, and our first range test got an alarm
through 350 m of buildings.

FloodMesh, week of 16 September. It is a small handheld for floods: press a
button and your message is passed from device to device until it reaches
someone who can help, with no phone network.

- Our own circuit board is designed. It is not built yet.
- First range test: one unit in a car, one at home, buildings in between.
  Alarms got through to about 350 m, then nothing.
- We wrote down how the network should behave in a real flood, including
  what a unit should do when its battery runs low.
- We started building in public. Every test result goes out, good or bad.

What did not work: we forgot to record signal strength during the range
test, so we cannot yet say why it stopped at 350 m. The next test will
record it.

This is early work. It runs on a bench, not yet in a flood. I am doing it
because I do not want another flood where a family cannot tell anyone they
need help.

If you work or volunteer with a disaster response team in Chennai: what is
the one message you most need from a family cut off in a flood?

Built in public.
