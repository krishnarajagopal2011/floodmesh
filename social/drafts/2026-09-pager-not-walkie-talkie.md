  idle most of the time instead of holding an open channel. README intro.

## Instagram caption

#discussion

We are building an off-grid pager for floods. It is a pager, not a
walkie-talkie.

A walkie-talkie needs a clear path between the two people talking. Flooded
streets full of buildings do not give you that. So this is a pager instead:
press a button and the message is held and passed from device to device,
building to building, until it reaches someone who can help. The radio does
not have to stay on all the time to do that.

If you were stuck, what would you want to send first: that you are safe,
that you need help, or where the water is?

Why: in Chennai 2015 and during Cyclone Michaung, towers and power failed
together. Neighbours 200 m apart could not reach each other.
FloodMesh passes alarms and 10-second voice notes between buildings with no
tower and no internet. It is designed to run for days on ordinary batteries.
Built in public.
