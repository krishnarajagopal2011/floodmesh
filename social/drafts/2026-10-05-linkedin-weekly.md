## Post

Our website, store and a tool to plan where units should go all moved
forward this week, and we still cannot read India's own radio rules for
ourselves.

FloodMesh, week of 29 September. It is a small handheld for floods: press
a button and your message is passed from device to device until it
reaches someone who can help, with no phone network.

- We built our public website: a home page, a pre-order store, and
  privacy and pre-order terms, in English, Tamil and Hindi.
- The store now shows volume discounts and packs sized for a street, a
  ward or a whole village, not just single units.
- We wrote up why we chose our current antenna, and a full checklist of
  every certificate and registration we expect to need before selling in
  India.
- We started a tool that plans where units should go on a map, and it can
  now show roughly how many households near each spot would actually get
  a message through.

What did not work: we tried to confirm India's exact radio rules for this
band this week and could not reach the government's own notice from
where we work. What we have is still an unverified secondhand summary.

If you read Tamil or Hindi: would you look at our new website and tell us
where the wording sounds wrong or unnatural?
