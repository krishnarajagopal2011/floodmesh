## Instagram caption

#trials #feedback

We are building an off-grid pager for floods. First range test: alarms got
through to about 350 m with buildings in between, then nothing.

That is not enough yet. But two devices with no network at all found each
other through a street of buildings, and that is where this starts. We did
not record signal strength, which we should have. Next round: record it on
every message, use the full power the law allows, and try a slower setting
that reaches further. Every result gets posted, the bad ones too.

If you were in a flood, how far away is the nearest person who could
actually help you? A neighbour, a volunteer post, a hospital?

Why: in Chennai 2015 and during Cyclone Michaung, towers and power failed
together. Neighbours 200 m apart could not reach each other.
FloodMesh passes alarms and 10-second voice notes between buildings with no
tower and no internet. It is designed to run for days on ordinary batteries.
Open hardware, built in public: link in bio.
